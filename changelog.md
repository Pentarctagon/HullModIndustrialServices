## 1.0.2
* Adds functionality to pay credits per ship to have an additional s-mod slot if the market has 100%+ ship quality. Does not stack with Best of the Best.

## 1.0.1
* Added the Hull Mod Services structure to Chicomoztoc, Kazeron, and Culann. Ship restoration cost will be reduced at those three locations. Requires a commission or a reputation of 50+ to use credits to add s-mods.
* Factions with over 100% ship quality will have s-mods added to their ships. This can be disabled via a Luna setting.
